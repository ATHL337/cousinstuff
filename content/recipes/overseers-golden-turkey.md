## Test status: draft

The citrus-garlic-herb butter and rub were used during our Thanksgiving cook and the family liked the flavor. The original whole bird roasted unevenly and the breast was a little dry. The exact applied amounts were not independently recorded. This draft reconstructs the planned seasonings and proposes a different cooking approach; **the sectioned method, yield and cook-time estimate have not been family-tested**. Allow extra time, and cook to temperature rather than the clock. Thawing time is excluded.

Use a calibrated food thermometer and check each section, particularly the shoulder meat. See [FoodSafety.gov roasting guidance](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/meat-poultry-charts). Wash and sanitize tools and surfaces after raw poultry contact.

Pair with [Liquid Gold Wasteland Gravy](/recipes/liquid-gold-wasteland-gravy/) and see the [holiday meal report](/wasteland-thanksgiving/).

Refrigerate cooked leftovers within two hours in shallow containers; use within 3–4 days or freeze. Reheat hot leftovers to 165°F.
