## Overview

The Vault 88 noodle counter doesn't have all day to simmer a traditional bone broth. It has a stockpot, a Ninja Foodi XL, an H-E-B supply run, and four hungry residents waiting for lunch.

**Vault 88 Brahmin Pho & Crispy Egg Rolls** creates a quick, fragrant, **pho-inspired** beef noodle soup using reduced-sodium beef broth, charred onion and ginger, modest amounts of Chinese five-spice and fish sauce, thin-shaved beef, flat rice noodles, and fresh garnishes. Frozen pork egg rolls cook in the Foodi while the broth develops.

This is an original quick family adaptation **inspired by Vietnamese phở bò**—not a traditional long-simmered pho. The Fallout naming is thematic.

**Theme:** Noodle Bar Rations  
**Profile:** Savory, gingery, gently spiced, brightened with lime and herbs  
**Vibe:** A crowded Vault 88 noodle counter feeding the settlement

**Test Kitchen status:** **Family-tested v1.0 — October 17, 2026.** All four first-batch tasters (BIFF, Kim, Ollie, and Isa) loved it. ## Yield & Portioning

- **Four hungry lunch diners:** Make four generous bowls using about **24 oz beef**, **10-12 oz dry noodles**, and **about 2 cups broth per bowl**; more beef is available if wanted. Air fry **8 egg rolls** (2 each). Refrigerate/freeze the **8 oz unused raw beef** promptly; reserve the other four egg rolls frozen. Leftover broth can become another quick meal.
- **Six standard family servings:** Use **all 32 oz beef**, **all 14 oz noodles**, **about 1 3/4-2 cups broth per bowl**, and **all 12 egg rolls** (2 each).
- Individual bowls can be smaller or larger. A smaller serving may use 3-4 oz raw-weight beef and fewer noodles, while a bigger bowl may take 6-7 oz or more, depending on appetite.

## Detailed Instructions

### 1. Start the Broth Aromatics

Keep the shaved beef in the refrigerator until you're ready to cook it. Cut **one yellow onion in half** and split **3 inches of ginger lengthwise**. Place cut sides down in a dry, hot skillet over medium-high heat. Char **5-7 minutes**, until deeply browned in spots (not completely burned).

### 2. Simmer the Shortcut Brahmin Stock

In a **6-8 qt stockpot**, combine:

- **3 × 32 oz reduced-sodium beef broth** = **96 oz / 12 cups**
- **Charred onion** and **ginger**
- **1/2 tsp Chinese five-spice**
- **1 tbsp fish sauce**
- **2 tsp sugar**

Bring to a simmer. Reduce heat and simmer gently **20-25 minutes**, loosely covered. Chinese five-spice isn't a one-for-one replacement for traditional pho spices, so keep the quantity restrained. **Do not add salt at the beginning**; broth and fish sauce already contain it.

### 3. Serve the Egg Rolls First While Stock Simmers

Put **8 frozen pork egg rolls** in the Ninja Foodi XL tray or basket for the four-person lunch, without overlapping them. Follow the **package's air-fryer instructions** for temperature, turning, and cooking time. Work in batches if they won't fit. Ensure centers are thoroughly hot (**165°F**, if checking by thermometer). **Serve and eat the egg rolls as soon as they are ready**, with sweet chili sauce. Keep the pho broth going during the appetizer so lunch service does not stall. For a six-person meal, cook all **12**.

### 4. Build the Garnish Station

Rinse basil, cilantro, and bean sprouts; slice green onions; quarter **4 limes**. Set out optional hoisin, Sriracha, and jalapeño separately. Keep the stockpot itself mild.

For children, pregnant diners, or anyone at higher risk from foodborne illness, **cook sprouts thoroughly** instead of serving them raw.

### 5. Cook Noodles Separately

Cook **10-12 oz dry noodles for four** or **14 oz for six** following the exact rice noodle package directions. Flat noodles can call for soaking rather than boiling. Drain immediately and distribute into large bowls. **Do not cook or store noodles in the broth**: they absorb liquid and get gummy.

### 6. Finish and Taste the Broth

Lift out the charred onion and ginger pieces. Taste the broth:

- Need more savory depth? Add **fish sauce 1 tsp at a time**, up to about 1 additional tablespoon.
- Need a little more aromatic spice? Add **a pinch of five-spice**, never more than **1/4 tsp additional** without tasting.
- Too salty? Add a little hot water or unsalted broth.
- Need brightness? Reserve the lime for the **individual bowl**, not the entire stockpot.

### 7. Fully Cook the Shaved Beef

Keep broth at a gentle simmer. Add the shaved beef in **2-3 batches**, separating clumps and stirring to cook evenly. Cook each batch until **fully cooked throughout**, with no raw centers. Timing depends on the cut, thickness, and how fast the pot returns to a simmer. Transfer cooked meat with clean utensils to a warm, covered bowl. Don't let raw-beef utensils touch cooked food.

This family recipe **does not rely on broth poured over raw beef to finish cooking**. It is cooked directly in the pot.

### 8. Assemble the Vault 88 Bowls

For **six standard servings**, aim for approximately:

- **2-2 1/3 oz dry noodle equivalent**, cooked
- **5 1/3 oz raw-weight shaved beef**, now cooked
- **1 3/4-2 cups hot broth**

For **four generous bowls**, begin with about **6 oz raw-weight beef** each, **2 1/2-3 oz dry noodle equivalent** each, and about **2 cups broth**; adjust according to appetite. Offer basil, cilantro, bean sprouts, green onion, lime, and sauces at the table.

## Availability / Substitution Protocols

| Missing ingredient | Best substitution |
|---|---|
| Chinese five-spice | **2 whole star anise + 1 cinnamon stick** in the broth for 25-30 min; remove before service; optional 2 cloves |
| Thai basil | Regular basil; flavor differs slightly |
| Shaved steak | Very thinly sliced boneless sirloin or eye of round, fully cooked |
| Bean sprouts | Extra green onions or a little finely shredded cabbage (not equivalent, but adds crunch) |
| Pork egg rolls | Frozen chicken or vegetable egg rolls, cooked per their own package |
| Fish sauce | In a pinch, use a little soy sauce, noting that the flavor will be less pho-like and may be saltier |

**Future upgrade:** Whole star anise and cinnamon (instead of five-spice) plus a **30-45-minute simmer** yield a flavor profile closer to traditional pho.

## Storage & Reheating

- **Prompt cooling:** Transfer broth and cooked beef to shallow containers and refrigerate **within 2 hours**. Keep noodles separate.
- **Broth + cooked beef:** Refrigerate **3-4 days** in airtight containers.
- **Cooked rice noodles:** Refrigerate separately and aim to use within **2-3 days** for best texture.
- **Uncooked shaved beef:** Refrigerate promptly and use within **1-2 days**, or freeze according to label guidance.
- **Uncooked frozen egg rolls:** Keep frozen according to package directions.
- **Reheat:** Heat cooked broth and beef until **165°F throughout**, then add separately warmed noodles just before serving.
- **Freeze:** Broth and cooked beef freeze well in serving-size containers for approximately **2-3 months for best quality**. Frozen cooked noodles tend to lose their texture.
