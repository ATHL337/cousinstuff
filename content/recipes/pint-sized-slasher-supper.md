## Overview

Something is stalking Appalachia, and apparently it expects dinner.

**The Pint-Sized Slasher Supper** turns the opening of Fallout 76's Slasher season into a real family meal for six. The centerpiece is **six individual mini meatloaves**, each marked with shallow knife slashes and finished with a glossy, dark-red glaze. Creamy mashed potatoes keep the plate firmly in pre-War comfort-food territory, blistered green beans bring the scorched edges, and Blood Pack Cherry Fizz completes the table.

The theme is deliberately creepy without making the food gross. No fake gore, no bizarre ingredients, and no sacrificing dinner for the joke. This should still be a meatloaf meal the family would want even if nobody at the table knew what a Pint-Sized Slasher was.

**Theme:** Core Rations  
**Profile:** Savory, sweet-tangy, creamy, lightly smoky, family-friendly  
**Vibe:** Pre-War comfort food served after somebody found a suspicious clown mask on the road to Sutton

## Kitchen Flow

The dinner works best when the mini meatloaves control the clock and everything else is staged around their short bake and rest.

| Mission Time | Action |
|---|---|
| **T-60** | Preheat oven to 400°F. Line the meatloaf pan. Prep onion, garlic, potatoes, and beans. |
| **T-50** | Mix meatloaf, divide into 6 portions, form mini loaves, score the slash marks, apply first glaze. |
| **T-35** | Mini meatloaves enter oven. Put potatoes over heat in cold salted water. |
| **T-25** | Potatoes should be approaching a simmer. Mix final glaze and stage green beans. |
| **T-20** | Pull mini meatloaves briefly, apply generous glaze, return to oven. Green beans enter oven. |
| **T-15** | Begin checking potatoes for tenderness. |
| **T-10** | Drain and mash potatoes. Keep covered and warm. Begin checking mini meatloaf temperature. |
| **T-08** | Remove mini meatloaves at 160°F and rest 5–10 minutes. |
| **T-05** | Pull green beans. Mix Blood Pack Cherry Fizz. |
| **T-00** | Slice, plate, photograph, and serve. |

The exact finish time can vary with the thickness of the mini loaves and the starting temperature of the beef. **Use internal temperature, not the clock, as the final authority.**

## Instructions

### 1. Preheat the Oven and Stage the Kitchen

Preheat the oven to **400°F**.

Line a rimmed sheet pan or large baking dish with parchment paper or foil.

Prep the vegetables before handling the ground beef:

- Finely dice the onion.
- Mince 5 garlic cloves, keeping 3 for the meatloaf and 2 for the potatoes.
- Peel the Yukon Gold potatoes and cut them into roughly 1 1/2-inch chunks.
- Trim the green beans.

Place the potatoes in a large pot and cover them with cold water by about an inch. Salt the water generously, but do not turn on the burner yet.

### 2. Build the Pint-Sized Slasher Mini Meatloaves

In a large bowl, add:

- Ground beef
- Diced onion
- 3 cloves minced garlic
- Breadcrumbs
- Eggs
- 1/3 cup milk
- 1 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 1/2 tbsp ketchup
- 2 tsp yellow mustard
- 1 1/4 tsp kosher salt
- 3/4 tsp black pepper
- 3/4 tsp smoked paprika
- 3/4 tsp dried parsley

Mix gently with clean hands or a large spoon until everything is evenly distributed.

Do not aggressively knead or compact the mixture. Overworking ground beef makes meatloaf dense.

Divide the mixture into **6 equal portions**, about 5 1/3 oz each before cooking.

Shape each portion into a compact oval mini loaf about the same size and thickness. Arrange all 6 on the prepared pan with at least 1 inch of space between them so hot air can circulate.

At about **5 1/3 oz raw per loaf**, these are substantial enough to anchor the plate without overwhelming the potatoes and green beans. For the cleanest timing, keep the loaves close to the same size. If you intentionally make a smaller child-sized loaf, begin checking that one early and remove it as soon as it reaches 160°F.

### 3. Leave the Slasher's Mark

Use a sharp knife to make **three shallow diagonal slashes** across the top of each mini loaf.

Keep the cuts decorative. About 1/4 inch deep is plenty. Deep cuts can cause the mini loaves to split while baking.

In a small bowl, combine:

- 3/4 cup ketchup
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder

Brush a **light first coat** over the tops and sides of all 6 mini loaves.

Bake at **400°F for 15 minutes**.

### 4. Start the Linden Street Potatoes

As soon as the meatloaf goes into the oven, place the covered potato pot over high heat.

Bring the water to a boil, then reduce to a steady simmer.

Cook for **15 to 18 minutes**, or until the potatoes are easily pierced with a fork.

Keep an eye on the potatoes while the mini meatloaves bake. The goal is to have them tender and ready to mash during the meatloaf rest.

### 5. Glaze the Evidence

After the mini meatloaves have baked for about 15 minutes, remove the pan from the oven.

Brush all 6 mini loaves generously with the remaining Slasher Glaze, letting some of it settle into the scored lines.

Return the pan to the oven.

Continue baking for **8 to 12 minutes**. Begin checking the center of the thickest mini loaf around **23 minutes total cooking time** with an instant-read thermometer.

The mini meatloaves are done when the centers reach **160°F**.

### 6. Roast the Scorched Green Beans

As soon as the mini meatloaves receive their second glaze, toss the green beans with:

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

Spread the beans in a single layer on a second pan.

Roast at **400°F for 12 to 15 minutes**, stirring once if convenient, until tender with browned and blistered spots.

For adults who want heat, add red pepper flakes to individual portions after cooking rather than making the family batch spicy.

### 7. Finish the Mashed Potatoes

Drain the tender potatoes thoroughly.

Return the empty pot to low heat and add the butter.

When the butter melts, add the remaining **2 cloves minced garlic** and cook for about **1 minute**, just until fragrant. Do not brown the garlic.

Return the potatoes to the pot.

Add:

- 3/4 cup warm milk
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 1/4 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

Mash until creamy.

Taste and adjust salt, pepper, milk, or butter as needed.

Cover and keep warm off the heat.

### 8. Rest the Mini Meatloaves

When the mini meatloaves reach **160°F**, remove them from the oven.

Let them rest for **5 to 10 minutes** before serving.

This is not dead time. Pull the green beans, mix the drink, clear the work area, and get the plate ready for the hero photograph.

### 9. Mix the Blood Pack Cherry Fizz

Fill a pitcher partway with ice.

Add:

- 4 cups cherry or black cherry juice
- 1 liter lemon-lime soda
- Lime slices

Stir gently.

Do this near serving time so the fizz stays lively.

### 10. Plate the Supper

Keep the rested mini meatloaves whole for serving. Each diner gets their own Pint-Sized Slasher.

For each plate:

1. Add a scoop of creamy mashed potatoes.
2. Place one whole mini meatloaf beside the potatoes with the red glaze and slash marks facing up.
3. Add a pile of blistered green beans.
4. Spoon a small amount of extra cooked glaze or pan juices over the mini meatloaf if desired.
5. Serve with a cold glass of Blood Pack Cherry Fizz.

## Vault Notes

### Why Six Mini Meatloaves?

A single 2-pound loaf works, but dividing the mixture into **6 individual mini loaves** is better for this recipe:

- Faster, more even cooking
- More glazed surface area
- Better portion control
- Six individual sets of visible Slasher marks
- Easier storage and sandwiches the next day

Most importantly, it makes the **"Pint-Sized"** part of the recipe literal: everybody gets their own little Slasher.

### Family Heat Control

Keep the main dinner mild.

Red pepper flakes belong at the table or on adult green-bean portions. The smoky paprika in the meatloaf adds flavor without making the meal hot.

### Lighter Plate Option

The recipe is intentionally written as a family comfort-food dinner, but individual plates can scale easily.

A lighter plate can use:

- One mini meatloaf, or half of one for a smaller appetite
- A smaller scoop of mashed potatoes
- A larger serving of green beans

Nobody needs a separate "diet dinner," and the rest of the family can serve themselves normally.

### Onion Texture

For anyone who dislikes noticeable onion pieces in meatloaf, grate the onion on the large holes of a box grater instead of dicing it.

The flavor stays, while the onion nearly disappears into the finished loaf.

### Do Not Fake the Gore

The glaze already provides all the visual theme the plate needs.

Avoid excessive food coloring or novelty "blood" effects. A glossy red meatloaf, visible slash marks, blistered vegetables, and a dark cherry drink sell the concept without making dinner unappetizing.

## Storage + Reheating

### Refrigerator

Store each component separately when possible.

- **Mini meatloaves:** airtight container for up to 4 days
- **Mashed potatoes:** airtight container for 3 to 4 days
- **Green beans:** airtight container for up to 3 days
- **Blood Pack Cherry Fizz:** best fresh; leftover mixed fizz will lose carbonation

### Freezer

Mini meatloaves freeze very well.

Let them cool, then freeze whole mini loaves or halved portions in airtight freezer containers or freezer bags for up to **3 months**.

Mashed potatoes can be frozen, but their texture is better fresh.

Roasted green beans are best refrigerated rather than frozen.

### Reheating

**Mini meatloaves:** microwave whole or halved portions in short intervals, or cover and warm in a 325°F oven until hot.

**Mashed potatoes:** microwave with a splash of milk, stirring between intervals. Add a little butter if they seem dry.

**Green beans:** reheat briefly in a skillet, toaster oven, or air fryer to recover some of their roasted texture.

## Leftover Field Rations

### Pint-Sized Slasher Meatloaf Sandwich

Toast sturdy sandwich bread and layer:

- Sliced warm mini meatloaf
- A thin swipe of extra ketchup or glaze
- Optional cheddar
- Pickles or sliced onion if desired

### Wasteland Breakfast Hash

Chop leftover mini meatloaf and potatoes.

Brown them together in a skillet and serve with eggs.

### Vault Lunch Bowl

Pack one leftover mini meatloaf, or half of one, with mashed potatoes and green beans in a microwave-safe container for an easy next-day lunch.

## Hero Photo Brief

The food should look like a real dinner first and themed food second.

### Primary Plate

Use a white, cream, gray, or otherwise neutral plate.

Build the plate with:

- Mashed potatoes at roughly 9 o'clock
- One whole glazed mini meatloaf across the center
- Green beans around 2 to 3 o'clock
- Glossy glaze facing the camera

Keep one mini meatloaf whole so the slash marks read clearly. For an interior shot, cut a second mini loaf in half and angle one half forward behind it.

### Drink Placement

Put the Blood Pack Cherry Fizz in a clear glass just behind the plate.

A lime wheel or wedge gives the drink enough contrast without turning it into a cocktail-production project.

### Best Hero Composition

Shoot from roughly a **45-degree angle**.

Keep:

- The plated meal in sharp focus
- Two or three additional marked mini meatloaves farther back in the frame
- The red drink visible but secondary
