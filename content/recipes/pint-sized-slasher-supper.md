## Overview

Something is stalking Appalachia, and apparently it expects dinner.

**The Pint-Sized Slasher Supper** turns the opening of Fallout 76's Slasher season into a real family meal for six. The centerpiece is a pair of tender meatloaves marked with shallow knife slashes and finished with a glossy, dark-red glaze. Creamy mashed potatoes keep the plate firmly in pre-War comfort-food territory, blistered green beans bring the scorched edges, and Blood Pack Cherry Fizz completes the table.

The theme is deliberately creepy without making the food gross. No fake gore, no bizarre ingredients, and no sacrificing dinner for the joke. This should still be a meatloaf meal the family would want even if nobody at the table knew what a Pint-Sized Slasher was.

**Theme:** Core Rations  
**Profile:** Savory, sweet-tangy, creamy, lightly smoky, family-friendly  
**Vibe:** Pre-War comfort food served after somebody found a suspicious clown mask on the road to Sutton

## Kitchen Flow

The dinner works best when the meatloaf controls the clock and everything else is staged around its final 25 minutes.

| Mission Time | Action |
|---|---|
| **T-75** | Preheat oven to 400°F. Line the meatloaf pan. Prep onion, garlic, potatoes, and beans. |
| **T-65** | Mix meatloaf, form two loaves, score the slash marks, apply first glaze. |
| **T-55** | Meatloaf enters oven. Put potatoes over heat in cold salted water. |
| **T-35** | Potatoes should be simmering. Mix final glaze and stage green beans. |
| **T-30** | Pull meatloaf briefly, apply generous glaze, return to oven. |
| **T-20** | Green beans enter oven. Begin checking potatoes for tenderness. |
| **T-15** | Drain and mash potatoes. Keep covered and warm. |
| **T-10** | Check meatloaf temperature. Remove at 160°F and rest. |
| **T-05** | Pull beans. Mix Blood Pack Cherry Fizz. |
| **T-00** | Slice, plate, photograph, and serve. |

The exact meatloaf finish time can vary with loaf thickness and the starting temperature of the beef. **Use internal temperature, not the clock, as the final authority.**

## Instructions

### 1. Preheat the Oven and Stage the Kitchen

Preheat the oven to **400°F**.

Line a rimmed sheet pan or large baking dish with parchment paper or foil.

Prep the vegetables before handling the ground beef:

- Finely dice the onion.
- Mince 5 garlic cloves, keeping 3 for the meatloaf and 2 for the potatoes.
- Peel the Yukon Gold potatoes and cut them into roughly 1 1/2-inch chunks.
- Trim the green beans.

Place the potatoes in a large pot and cover them with cold water by about an inch. Salt the water generously, but do not turn on the burner yet.

### 2. Build the Pint-Sized Slasher Meatloaves

In a large bowl, add:

- Ground beef
- Diced onion
- 3 cloves minced garlic
- Breadcrumbs
- Eggs
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp ketchup
- Yellow mustard
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- Smoked paprika
- Dried parsley

Mix gently with clean hands or a large spoon until everything is evenly distributed.

Do not aggressively knead or compact the mixture. Overworking ground beef makes meatloaf dense.

Divide the mixture into **two equal portions**.

Shape each portion into a compact free-form loaf about the same size and thickness. Leave several inches of space between them on the pan.

### 3. Leave the Slasher's Mark

Use a sharp knife to make **three shallow diagonal slashes** across the top of each loaf.

Keep the cuts decorative. About 1/4 inch deep is plenty. Deep cuts can cause the loaves to separate while baking.

In a small bowl, combine:

- 3/4 cup ketchup
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder

Brush a **light first coat** over the tops and sides of both loaves.

Bake at **400°F for 25 minutes**.

### 4. Start the Linden Street Potatoes

As soon as the meatloaf goes into the oven, place the covered potato pot over high heat.

Bring the water to a boil, then reduce to a steady simmer.

Cook for **15 to 18 minutes**, or until the potatoes are easily pierced with a fork.

Do not drain them until the meatloaf has received its second glaze. The goal is to keep the sides synchronized with the main dish.

### 5. Glaze the Evidence

After the meatloaves have baked for about 25 minutes, remove the pan from the oven.

Brush both loaves generously with the remaining Slasher Glaze, letting some of it settle into the scored lines.

Return the pan to the oven.

Continue baking for **15 to 25 minutes**, checking the center of the thickest loaf with an instant-read thermometer.

The meatloaf is done when the center reaches **160°F**.

### 6. Roast the Scorched Green Beans

While the glazed meatloaf finishes, toss the green beans with:

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

Spread the beans in a single layer on a second pan.

Roast at **400°F for 12 to 15 minutes**, stirring once if convenient, until tender with browned and blistered spots.

For adults who want heat, add red pepper flakes to individual portions after cooking rather than making the family batch spicy.

### 7. Finish the Mashed Potatoes

Drain the tender potatoes thoroughly.

Return the empty pot to low heat and add the butter.

When the butter melts, add the remaining **2 cloves minced garlic** and cook for about **1 minute**, just until fragrant. Do not brown the garlic.

Return the potatoes to the pot.

Add:

- 3/4 cup warm milk
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

Mash until creamy.

Taste and adjust salt, pepper, milk, or butter as needed.

Cover and keep warm off the heat.

### 8. Rest the Meatloaf

When the meatloaves reach **160°F**, remove them from the oven.

Let them rest for **10 minutes** before slicing.

This is not dead time. Pull the green beans, mix the drink, clear the work area, and get the plate ready for the hero photograph.

### 9. Mix the Blood Pack Cherry Fizz

Fill a pitcher partway with ice.

Add:

- 4 cups cherry or black cherry juice
- 1 liter lemon-lime soda
- Lime slices

Stir gently.

Do this near serving time so the fizz stays lively.

### 10. Plate the Supper

Cut the rested meatloaf into thick slices.

For each plate:

1. Add a scoop of creamy mashed potatoes.
2. Place one or two meatloaf slices beside the potatoes with the red glaze facing up.
3. Add a pile of blistered green beans.
4. Spoon a small amount of extra cooked glaze or pan juices over the meatloaf if desired.
5. Serve with a cold glass of Blood Pack Cherry Fizz.

## Vault Notes

### Why Two Meatloaves?

A single 2 1/2-pound loaf becomes a large thermal mass. Dividing the mixture into two loaves gives you:

- Faster, more even cooking
- More glazed surface area
- Better portion control
- Two sets of visible Slasher marks
- Easier storage and sandwiches the next day

It also makes the "Pint-Sized" part of the recipe name actually mean something.

### Family Heat Control

Keep the main dinner mild.

Red pepper flakes belong at the table or on adult green-bean portions. The smoky paprika in the meatloaf adds flavor without making the meal hot.

### Lighter Plate Option

The recipe is intentionally written as a family comfort-food dinner, but individual plates can scale easily.

A lighter plate can use:

- One meatloaf slice
- A smaller scoop of mashed potatoes
- A larger serving of green beans

Nobody needs a separate "diet dinner," and the rest of the family can serve themselves normally.

### Onion Texture

For anyone who dislikes noticeable onion pieces in meatloaf, grate the onion on the large holes of a box grater instead of dicing it.

The flavor stays, while the onion nearly disappears into the finished loaf.

### Do Not Fake the Gore

The glaze already provides all the visual theme the plate needs.

Avoid excessive food coloring or novelty "blood" effects. A glossy red meatloaf, visible slash marks, blistered vegetables, and a dark cherry drink sell the concept without making dinner unappetizing.

## Storage + Reheating

### Refrigerator

Store each component separately when possible.

- **Meatloaf:** airtight container for up to 4 days
- **Mashed potatoes:** airtight container for 3 to 4 days
- **Green beans:** airtight container for up to 3 days
- **Blood Pack Cherry Fizz:** best fresh; leftover mixed fizz will lose carbonation

### Freezer

Meatloaf freezes very well.

Let it cool, slice it, and freeze individual portions in airtight freezer containers or freezer bags for up to **3 months**.

Mashed potatoes can be frozen, but their texture is better fresh.

Roasted green beans are best refrigerated rather than frozen.

### Reheating

**Meatloaf:** microwave individual slices in short intervals, or cover and warm in a 325°F oven until hot.

**Mashed potatoes:** microwave with a splash of milk, stirring between intervals. Add a little butter if they seem dry.

**Green beans:** reheat briefly in a skillet, toaster oven, or air fryer to recover some of their roasted texture.

## Hero Photo Brief

The food should look like a real dinner first and themed food second.

### Primary Plate

Use a white, cream, gray, or otherwise neutral plate.

Build the plate with:

- Mashed potatoes at roughly 9 o'clock
- Sliced meatloaf across the center
- Green beans around 2 to 3 o'clock
- Glossy glaze facing the camera

Angle one meatloaf slice slightly forward so the interior is visible while the marked top of the remaining loaf can still appear in the background.

### Drink Placement

Put the Blood Pack Cherry Fizz in a clear glass just behind the plate.

A lime wheel or wedge gives the drink enough contrast without turning it into a cocktail-production project.

### Best Hero Composition

Shoot from roughly a **45-degree angle**.

Keep:

- The plated meal in sharp focus
- The remaining marked meatloaf farther back in the frame
- The red drink visible but secondary
- A little open space around the food for the CousinStuff layout
