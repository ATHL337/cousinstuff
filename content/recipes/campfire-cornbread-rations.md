## Overview

Campfire Cornbread Rations are a fast Fallout-friendly side for soups, stews, chili, and chowder. The corn and cheddar make the boxed mix feel more like a real dinner side, while honey and butter give it that warm campfire comfort.

**Theme:** Campfire Sides  
**Profile:** Warm, buttery, lightly sweet, savory  
**Vibe:** Simple settlement food cooked in a cast iron pan  

## Ingredients

### Core Side

- 1 box cornbread mix, such as Jiffy
- 1 egg
- 1/3 cup milk
- 1/2 cup corn
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp butter, melted

## Instructions

### 1. Preheat the Oven

Heat oven to **400°F**.

Grease an 8x8 baking dish or cast iron skillet.

### 2. Mix the Batter

In a bowl, combine:

- Cornbread mix
- Egg
- Milk
- Corn
- Cheddar
- Honey
- Melted butter

Stir just until combined. Do not overmix.

### 3. Bake

Pour the batter into the prepared dish.

Bake for **18–22 minutes**, until golden and set in the center.

### 4. Rest and Serve

Let the cornbread rest for 5 minutes before slicing.

Serve warm.

## Vault Notes

### Best Pairings

This side works especially well with:

- Mirelurk Special Texas Wasteland Crab and Beer Chowder
- Chili
- Bean soup
- Grilled meats
- Barbecue chicken
- Any thick stew that needs something to scoop with

### Cut-Fit Switches

Pick based on the day:

- **Need less sweet:** skip the honey.
- **Need more savory:** add diced jalapeño or extra cheddar.
- **Need more kid-friendly:** keep it plain with corn and cheddar.
- **Need more wasteland:** bake it in cast iron.

## Storage + Reheating

- **Room temp:** keep covered up to 1 day.
- **Fridge:** store airtight up to 4 days.
- **Reheat:** microwave slices for 10–20 seconds or warm in a low oven.
- **Best texture:** serve the same day if possible.
