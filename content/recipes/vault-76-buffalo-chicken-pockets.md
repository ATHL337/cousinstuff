## Overview

These **Vault 76 Crispy Buffalo Chicken Pockets** convert a social-media rice-paper hot-pocket idea into a repeatable CousinStuff family dinner: **measured ingredients, exactly 12 pockets, an oven-first family batch, and heat controlled before assembly**. Each adult-size serving is **2 pockets**, with a salad or crunchy vegetables on the side.

**Theme:** Vault Field Rations  
**Profile:** High-protein, crispy outside, creamy chicken-and-cheese center  
**Method:** Oven first; Ninja Foodi XL/air-fryer alternative below  
**Yield:** 12 pockets, about 6 two-pocket servings  
**Status:** Recipe development draft; not yet family-tested

This recipe assumes the chicken is **already fully cooked**. Cook and cool raw chicken separately before starting; the published prep time does not include that step.

## Equipment

- Large mixing bowl plus a second bowl for the spicy half
- Blender or immersion blender for cottage cheese
- Two rimmed baking sheets (or one sheet cooked in two rounds)
- Parchment rated for 425°F, or lightly oiled baking sheets
- Wide shallow dish for warm water, pastry brush, tongs or thin spatula
- Instant-read thermometer and wire cooling rack
- Optional: Ninja Foodi XL or other basket-style air fryer

## Ingredients and Family Heat Split

### Creamy Chicken Base

- **1½ lb (680 g) cooked shredded chicken breast**, weighed **after** cooking
- **8 oz (227 g) low-fat cottage cheese**, blended smooth
- **¾ cup (about 85 g) part-skim shredded mozzarella**
- **¼ cup green onions**, finely chopped
- **1 tsp onion powder**
- **¼ tsp black pepper**
- **Salt only if needed**, since the cheese and buffalo sauce already contribute sodium

### Two Flavor Loads

| Batch | Approximate yield | Add to half the filling |
|---|---:|---|
| **Vault-Safe Mild** | 6 pockets | No hot sauce, or at most 1 tsp buffalo sauce |
| **Buffalo Afterburn** | 6 pockets | 3 tbsp Frank's RedHot **Buffalo Wings Sauce**; add more at the table if desired |

Using the Buffalo Wings Sauce rather than straight original cayenne hot sauce matters because their flavor and thickness differ. Either will work, but adjust to taste.

### Wrappers and Finish

- **24 large round rice-paper sheets**, about 8½–9 inches (22 cm), **2 per pocket**
- **1 large egg**, beaten, for brushing
- **Warm water**, for softening rice paper
- **Light neutral-oil spray**
- Ranch, blue cheese dressing, or extra buffalo sauce for serving, optional

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Build the Creamy Protein Filling

Blend **8 oz cottage cheese** until smooth. This helps it spread through the shredded chicken instead of leaving large curds.

In a large bowl, fold together the **1½ lb fully cooked shredded chicken**, smooth cottage cheese, **¾ cup mozzarella**, **¼ cup green onions**, **1 tsp onion powder**, and **¼ tsp pepper**. Keep the mixture thick enough to mound on a spoon; if watery, drain excess liquid rather than adding more sauce.

### 2. Split the Heat Before Wrapping

Divide the filling evenly into two bowls.

- **Mild bowl:** leave plain or add **up to 1 tsp** buffalo sauce.
- **Spicy bowl:** add **3 tbsp** Frank's RedHot Buffalo Wings Sauce, mixing until evenly coated.

This keeps half of dinner mild without sacrificing the adults' buffalo flavor. Keep the finished pockets in separate zones on the tray.

### 3. Soften and Double-Wrap

Fill a shallow dish with warm water. Dip **one rice-paper wrapper for 1–2 seconds** and lay it on a clean, lightly damp work surface. Repeat with a second wrapper and stack them. Allow a few seconds for flexibility.

**Do not leave wrappers soaking.** They continue to soften as you assemble. Water-softening followed by an egg wash is easier to control than trying to fully hydrate the sheets in beaten egg.

### 4. Portion and Fold

Place **about ½ cup filling** in a compact rectangle near the center. Fold in opposite sides, then the remaining sides to form a flat, fully enclosed pocket. Press overlaps lightly to seal.

Transfer **seam-side down** to the baking sheet. Repeat for **12 pockets**. If your rice-paper brand is smaller than 8½ inches, use less filling per pocket rather than forcing the seal; you may get more than 12 smaller pockets and need additional wrappers.

### 5. Oven-Crisp the Family Batch

Brush the pockets lightly with beaten egg, then mist with oil. Leave **at least 1 inch** between them.

Bake at **425°F (220°C)** for **15–20 minutes**, carefully turning after about 10 minutes and swapping sheet positions if using two pans. The wrappers should be golden and crisp. Confirm the center of a pocket reaches **165°F (74°C)**; if not, continue baking in short intervals.

**Do not broil on parchment.** The inspiration recipe uses a 450°F bake followed by a long broil; CousinStuff uses a controlled 425°F bake instead to reduce burning risk and give thicker family-size pockets time to heat through.

### 6. Rest and Serve

Let the pockets rest **3–5 minutes on a wire rack**, which keeps trapped steam from softening the undersides. Serve **2 pockets per person** with carrot sticks, celery, cucumbers, or a chopped salad. Put ranch, blue cheese dressing, and extra buffalo sauce on the table instead of inside the whole batch.

## Ninja Foodi XL / Air-Fryer Alternative

Use this for a **small test batch or reheating**, not as the fastest method for all 12 pockets.

1. Preheat to **375°F (190°C)**.
2. Arrange pockets in a lightly oiled basket **in a single layer**, with space between them.
3. Brush lightly with egg and mist with oil.
4. Air-fry **10–14 minutes**, turning around halfway. Check for crisp wrappers and a **165°F center**. Extend in short increments if needed.
5. Repeat in batches, keeping the rest refrigerated until ready to cook.

Air-fryer models and pocket thickness vary. Treat the timing as a starting point, not a guaranteed finish.

### Troubleshooting

- **Wrappers tear:** wet for less time, handle with damp hands, and reduce filling slightly.
- **Pockets stick:** use lightly oiled parchment or metal; allow the underside to set before turning.
- **Wrappers stay chewy:** increase airflow and spacing, use a lighter egg coating, and bake until the surface is fully crisp.
- **Filling leaks:** fold tighter, place seam-side down initially, and do not overfill.
- **Too spicy:** keep the base filling mild and offer buffalo sauce after cooking.
- **Filling too wet:** drain watery cottage cheese before blending, and avoid adding excess sauce.

## Storage + Reheating

- **Refrigerate:** Cool promptly, refrigerate within **2 hours**, and use within **3–4 days** at 40°F or colder. If using older cooked chicken, count storage time from when the chicken was originally cooked, not from when the pockets were assembled.
- **Reheat in oven:** Use **375°F** for about **8–12 minutes**, longer if very cold, until the center reaches **165°F**.
- **Reheat in air fryer:** Start at **350°F** for **5–8 minutes**, then check the center for **165°F**.
- **Microwave:** Safe if fully reheated, but the rice-paper shell will soften rather than stay crisp.
- **Make ahead:** Prepare and chill the filling one day ahead, then wrap shortly before cooking. Uncooked wrapped rice paper can become sticky when refrigerated.
- **Freezer:** Best to freeze the cooked chicken filling separately and assemble fresh pockets later. Freezing fully assembled pockets may compromise the wrapper texture.
