# Rotisserie Chicken Shin Black Ramen

This is a fast, memorable family ramen dinner built from instant ramen, then upgraded with low-sodium chicken broth, rotisserie chicken, fresh vegetables, soft-boiled eggs, and simple toppings.

The main pot stays family-friendly, while adults can upgrade their own bowls with chili crisp, Kewpie mayo, seaweed, or extra spice.

## Why This Works

Shin Ramyun Black brings a richer broth base than standard instant ramen. The 33% less sodium chicken broth adds body without pushing the salt level too far. Rotisserie chicken keeps the meal fast, easy, and protein-forward.

This turns “instant noodles” into an actual family dinner.

## Yield

- Serves: 4 to 5
- Time: 25 to 35 minutes
- Best for: Weeknight dinner, family ramen night, quick comfort food

## Ingredients

### Core Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Nongshim Shin Ramyun Black | 4 packs | Use noodles, broth packets, and adjustable spice packets |
| 33% less sodium chicken broth | 4 cups | Broth booster |
| Water | 6 cups | Total liquid should be about 10 cups |
| Rotisserie chicken | 2 to 3 cups shredded | About half of a 2 lb chicken |
| Eggs | 4 to 6 | Soft-boiled or medium-boiled |
| Sliced mushrooms | 1 pack | Add to broth |
| Matchstick carrots | 1 bag | Add to broth |
| Baby spinach or bok choy | 1 bag | Add near the end |
| Green onions | 1 bunch | Topping |
| Lime | 1 | Cut into wedges |

### Optional Bowl Upgrades

| Ingredient | Use |
|---|---|
| Kewpie mayo | Creamy adult ramen base |
| Chili crisp or chili oil | Adult heat and crunch |
| Seaweed snacks or nori | Tear into strips as topping |
| Furikake or sesame seeds | Finishing sprinkle |
| Kimchi | Side or topping |
| Frozen potstickers | Easy side dish |

## Spice Control

Use all of the Shin Black bone broth or base packets.

For the red spicy packets:

| Spice Level | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Mild family pot | 2 packets |
| Medium | 3 packets |
| Spicy | 4 packets |

The best family approach is to use 2 spicy packets in the main pot, then let adults add chili crisp or extra spice to their own bowls.

## Instructions

### 1. Soft-Boil the Eggs

Bring a small pot of water to a boil.

Add the eggs carefully.

Cook:

- 6 minutes for jammy yolks
- 8 minutes for firmer yolks

Move the eggs to cold water, peel them, and set them aside.

Slice in half before serving.

### 2. Prep the Chicken and Toppings

Shred about 2 to 3 cups of rotisserie chicken.

Slice the green onions.

Cut the lime into wedges.

Open and stage the mushrooms, carrots, and spinach or bok choy.

Set out optional toppings like chili crisp, seaweed snacks, Kewpie mayo, furikake, or sesame seeds.

### 3. Build the Broth

In a large pot, add:

- 4 cups chicken broth
- 6 cups water
- Shin Black broth or base packets
- 2 to 3 red spicy packets, depending on family heat tolerance

Bring to a simmer.

### 4. Add the Vegetables

Add the sliced mushrooms and matchstick carrots.

Simmer for 3 to 4 minutes.

Add the spinach or bok choy and cook for 1 more minute.

### 5. Cook the Noodles

Add the ramen noodles directly to the simmering broth.

Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring gently.

Do not overcook. Serve immediately so the noodles do not get soggy.

### 6. Warm the Chicken

Add the shredded rotisserie chicken during the final 1 to 2 minutes.

Do not hard-boil the chicken. It is already cooked and only needs to warm through.

### 7. Assemble the Bowls

Each bowl gets:

1. Noodles
2. Broth
3. Vegetables
4. Rotisserie chicken
5. Egg halves
6. Green onions
7. Lime squeeze
8. Optional toppings

## Optional Creamy Kewpie Bowl Method

Do this per bowl, not in the main pot.

Add 1 tablespoon Kewpie mayo to the serving bowl.

Add a small ladle of hot broth.

Whisk until smooth.

Add noodles, more broth, chicken, vegetables, egg, and toppings.

This creates a richer, creamier ramen-shop style bowl without changing the main pot for everyone.

## Notes

Do not add soy sauce unless the broth tastes weak. The ramen packets already contain plenty of sodium.

Do not boil the rotisserie chicken for long or it may become dry and stringy.

Seaweed snacks should be added at the end so they keep some texture.

Lime is a strong finishing move because it brightens the broth and cuts the richness.

Save leftover chicken for quesadillas, wraps, rice bowls, or chicken salad.

## Final Recommended Version

For the clean family dinner version, use:

- Shin Black ramen
- 33% less sodium chicken broth
- Water
- Rotisserie chicken
- Eggs
- Mushrooms
- Matchstick carrots
- Spinach or bok choy
- Green onions
- Lime

For adult upgrades, use:

- Kewpie mayo
