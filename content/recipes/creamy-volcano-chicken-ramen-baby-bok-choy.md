## Overview

Paldo Volcano Chicken Noodle is sold as a **spicy stir-fry noodle**, but it makes a surprisingly satisfying **creamy chicken ramen soup** when paired with a separate chicken-shiitake broth. This family version trades the package's drain-and-sauce method for **four generous, extra-brothy bowls** with tender baby bok choy, carrots, fresh mushrooms, and a rich sauce-and-mayo base whisked directly in each serving bowl.

**Family-tested and loved:** The cook, their spouse, and their two oldest children all enjoyed this creamy Volcano ramen. The individual bowl method was a keeper: everyone gets a smooth, rich broth without needing to emulsify mayonnaise into a crowded stockpot.

**Flavor profile:** Spicy chicken, savory shiitake, toasted sesame, silky egg-enriched broth, crisp-tender bok choy.

**Important distinction:** This is a deliberate **soup adaptation** of Paldo Volcano Chicken Noodle, not the manufacturer's stir-fry preparation. The Paldo **liquid sauce packets** go into the serving bowls, not the shared pot.

## Equipment

- Large 6–8 quart stockpot
- Small saucepan and bowl of ice water
- Skillet with lid for dumplings, if the package calls for skillet steaming
- Four large, heatproof ramen bowls
- Knife, cutting board, ladle, whisk or fork, and tongs

## Ingredients

### Shared Chicken-Shiitake Broth

- **4 packages Paldo Volcano Chicken Noodle**, noodle blocks separated from their **4 liquid sauce packets**
- **12 cups water** for the family's preferred extra-brothy version
- **Chicken bouillon sufficient for 6 cups at the manufacturer's normal strength** (making the full 12 cups approximately half-strength)
- **6–8 oz fresh shiitake mushrooms**, sliced
- **2 small heads baby bok choy**, washed, stems and leaves separated
- **1½ cups carrot matchsticks**
- **2 tsp garlic powder**
- **4 green onions**, thinly sliced

### Creamy Volcano Base — Each Bowl

- **1 Paldo Volcano liquid sauce packet**
- **1 tbsp mayonnaise**
- **1 pasteurized egg yolk**
- **¼ tsp toasted sesame oil**

**Four-bowl total:** 4 sauce packets, 4 tbsp mayonnaise, 4 pasteurized yolks, 1 tsp sesame oil.

### Jammy Eggs & Dumplings

- **4 additional eggs**, one jammy egg per serving
- **12 frozen chicken dumplings**, three per serving
- Optional: rice vinegar to brighten finished bowls

Optional dumpling dip: **2 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tbsp rice vinegar + ½ tsp sesame oil**.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Baby Bok Choy

Wash the baby bok choy thoroughly, especially near the base where grit can hide. Cut the firmer pale stems into bite-size pieces and roughly chop the leaves. **Keep stems and leaves separate**: stems need several minutes in the broth, while the leaves wilt quickly.

Slice the shiitakes, set out the carrot matchsticks, and thinly slice the green onions for garnish.

### 2. Make the Jammy Eggs

Bring a small pot of water to a boil. Carefully lower in **4 eggs** and cook for **about 7 minutes** for jammy centers (adjust slightly for egg size and preference). Transfer immediately to ice water. Peel and halve just before serving.

These are separate from the **4 pasteurized yolks** used for the creamy bases.

### 3. Cook Dumplings Separately

Prepare **12 frozen chicken dumplings** according to their package instructions. Use the skillet-and-steam method if indicated, so the dumplings develop browned bottoms. Keep them warm and serve **three per person** alongside the ramen.

Do not boil them in the shared ramen broth; keeping them separate preserves their texture.

### 4. Build the Extra-Brothy Chicken-Shiitake Stock

In a large stockpot combine:

- **12 cups water**
- Bouillon sufficient to season **6 cups** at its normal strength
- **6–8 oz sliced shiitakes**
- **Baby bok choy stems**
- **2 tsp garlic powder**

Bring to a boil, reduce to a lively simmer, and cook for **4 minutes**. The broth should taste lightly savory, not heavily salted: Paldo's liquid sauce adds concentrated flavor later.

**Do not add the Paldo liquid sauce to this pot.**

### 5. Cook Noodles and Finish Vegetables

Add **all four Paldo noodle blocks** to the simmering broth. Gently separate them as they soften.

After about **2 minutes**, add the **carrot matchsticks and baby bok choy leaves**. Continue for **1–1½ minutes**, aiming for the package's **3 minutes 30 seconds total** noodle cooking time.

Remove the pot from the heat while the noodles are still a touch firm. **Do not drain**; this is a soup conversion.

### 6. Whisk the Creamy Volcano Bases

Set out **four large heatproof bowls**. In **each** bowl combine:

- 1 **Paldo liquid sauce packet**
- 1 tbsp **mayonnaise**
- 1 **pasteurized egg yolk**
- ¼ tsp **sesame oil**

Whisk until smooth. Slowly add **½ cup very hot broth**, whisking constantly, followed by another **½ cup**. This makes a creamy, spicy base without clumps.

**Food safety:** Tempering with hot broth is not a validated method of cooking an unpasteurized raw yolk. Use **pasteurized yolks** for this creamy preparation, particularly when serving children.

### 7. Assemble Four Extra-Brothy Bowls

Divide noodles, mushrooms, carrots, and bok choy evenly among the bowls. Ladle in the remaining broth, stirring from the bottom to incorporate the creamy base. Aim for **about 2½ cups broth per bowl**, depending on evaporation and bowl capacity.

If serving bowls are small, add broth in stages or serve extra in a heatproof pitcher.

### 8. Finish and Serve

Peel and halve the four jammy eggs. Top each ramen bowl with **two egg halves** and plenty of **sliced green onion**.

Taste before seasoning further. If the rich broth needs brightness, stir in **¼ tsp rice vinegar per bowl**. Avoid automatic additions of soy sauce, butter, or extra bouillon; the Paldo sauce, mayo, yolk, and chicken stock already supply plenty of flavor.

Serve the **crispy chicken dumplings separately** with the optional soy-vinegar-sesame dipping sauce.

## Why This Version Works

- **Baby bok choy** gives fresh crunch and a leafy contrast to the spicy broth. Its stems get a head start, while its leaves cook briefly.
- **Shiitake mushrooms** provide savory depth to the large quantity of soup.
- **Half-strength bouillon** supports the extra liquid without asking four stir-fry sauce packets to flavor twelve cups of plain water alone.
- **Individual creamy bases** let each serving emulsify its own Paldo sauce, mayo, yolk, and sesame oil.
- **Jammy eggs and separately cooked dumplings** make the meal substantial without crowding the stockpot.

## Family Field Notes

**Family-approved:** This creamy Paldo adaptation earned enthusiastic approval from the cook, their spouse, and their two oldest children. The combination worth repeating is **fresh baby bok choy + shiitake broth + creamy individual bowl bases**.

**More broth was the requested improvement.** The 12-cup starting volume reflects that preference; the final amount served depends on evaporation and how much broth each bowl can hold.

**Spice control:** Paldo Volcano is intentionally hot. Start with less of the liquid sauce in a heat-sensitive diner's bowl, then add the rest to taste. If using less sauce, the resulting bowl will be milder and less intensely seasoned.

**Storage:** Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Keep noodles and broth separate when possible to prevent sogginess. For best texture, prepare the creamy bases fresh at serving time.
