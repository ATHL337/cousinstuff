## Overview

Vault-Tec Field Greens with Mutfruit Slices are the fresh counterweight to heavy Fallout comfort food. The apple stands in for mutfruit, the cucumber and carrot add crunch, and the honey-Dijon cider dressing keeps the salad bright without making it fussy.

**Theme:** Vault-Tec Field Rations  
**Profile:** Crisp, fresh, lightly sweet, acidic  
**Vibe:** A clean vault cafeteria side trying its best during post-war supply chain collapse  

## Ingredients

### Salad

- 6 cups spring mix or romaine
- 1 apple, sliced thin
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- 1 carrot, shredded or shaved
- 1/4 cup sunflower seeds or chopped pecans
- 1/3 cup feta or cheddar cubes

### Honey-Dijon Cider Dressing

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 1/2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- Salt, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

### 1. Prep the Salad

Add the greens, apple slices, cucumber, carrot, sunflower seeds or pecans, and cheese to a large bowl.

### 2. Make the Dressing

In a small bowl or jar, combine:

- Olive oil
- Apple cider vinegar
- Honey
- Dijon mustard
- Salt
- Black pepper

Whisk or shake until combined.

### 3. Toss and Serve

Toss the salad lightly with dressing right before serving.

Do not overdress. The goal is crisp field greens, not soggy vault compost.

## Vault Notes

### Mutfruit Options

Apple is the best everyday mutfruit stand-in, but you can also use:

- Pear slices
- Dried cranberries
- Grapes
- Orange segments
- Berries

### Kid / Adult Split

Keep the main salad simple with apple, cucumber, carrot, and cheese.

Adults can add:

- Extra black pepper
- Pickled onions
- Pecans
- Blue cheese
- Chili honey

### Cut-Fit Switches

Pick based on the day:

- **Need simpler:** greens, apple, cucumber, dressing.
- **Need more protein:** add grilled chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or chickpeas.
- **Need more crunch:** add croutons, pecans, sunflower seeds, or crispy onions.
- **Need dairy-free:** skip the cheese.
- **Need sweeter:** add dried cranberries.

## Storage + Reheating

- **Best fresh:** toss right before serving.
- **Prep ahead:** slice vegetables and make dressing earlier in the day.
- **Prevent browning:** toss apple slices with a little lemon juice.
- **Leftovers:** dressed salad is best eaten the same day.
