## Overview

When the Vault needs a holiday morale boost, this is the dessert to requisition. A lightly spiced pumpkin custard bakes underneath a buttery golden cake topping, without making pie crust or preparing cake batter. It is meant to be spooned out, warm and comforting.

**Theme:** Sugar Rations  
**Profile:** Family-friendly, holiday dessert, easy assembly  
**Vibe:** Pumpkin pie meets buttery crumble cake  

**Equipment:** 9×13-inch baking dish, large mixing bowl, whisk, spatula, and wire rack.

## Vault Notes

### Why the Layers Matter

- **Use pure pumpkin purée.** Pumpkin pie filling already contains sugar and spices, and will change the sweetness and texture.
- **Keep the cake mix dry.** Sprinkle it directly over the pumpkin mixture; do not follow the cake box's egg, oil, or water directions.
- **Make the butter slices very thin.** The goal is to moisten the whole cake-mix surface as the butter melts. Avoid thick slices and uncovered patches of powder.
- **Do not stir after layering.** The separate custard and cake layers are what make this a dump cake.

### Family / Holiday Upgrades

- **Extra crunch:** add the optional chopped pecans before baking. Leave them off for a nut-free topping, checking package labels if allergies are a concern.
- **More aromatic:** substitute 2 1/2 tsp pumpkin pie spice for the cinnamon and nutmeg together.
- **For a party:** cut into 12 standard portions, or 10 generous ones. Serve with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream.
- **For a firmer square:** let it cool longer, then chill before slicing. Warm portions briefly to serve.

## Doneness and Troubleshooting

- **Center looks wet or sloshes:** keep baking in 5-minute increments and check again.
- **Golden topping with small soft spots:** normal; the topping is a buttery crumble, not a conventional layer cake.
- **Loose, powdery cake mix on top:** cover those patches with a few additional thin butter shavings if available, then return to the oven briefly.
- **Top browning too fast:** tent loosely with aluminum foil for the remaining baking time.

## Storage + Reheating

- **Best fresh:** serve warm after the 20-minute rest.
- **Fridge:** cover and refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours; keep for up to 3–4 days.
- **Reheat:** microwave an individual portion in short 20–30-second intervals until warm, or warm covered in a low oven.
- **Make ahead:** bake the day before, refrigerate, and reheat portions just before serving.

## Serve With

- Vanilla ice cream
- Lightly sweetened whipped cream
- Coffee or spiced tea

## Recipe Standard Notes

This version specifies a 29-oz pumpkin base, a 15.25-oz cake mix, and an even butter topping for a full 9×13-inch pan. Package sizes vary by brand; for repeatable results, check the printed weights rather than relying on "one box" alone.

**Timing:** about 15 minutes prep, 55–65 minutes baking, plus at least 20 minutes cooling. The listed total time uses a 60-minute bake and 20-minute rest.
